Privacy policy

Updated October 23, 2026

This policy describes a private, owner-operated OpenClaw integration. It does not describe the OpenClaw project or the gog project as a whole.

Data accessed and purpose

With the account owner's authorization, the integration accesses Gmail messages and attachments, calendar events, Drive files and metadata, Google Docs, and Google Sheets. It uses this information to perform requested personal-assistant tasks, such as answering questions, summarizing information, drafting email, managing schedules, and working with documents or spreadsheets. The permissions granted during Google consent determine the available access.

Processing and sharing

The gog CLI calls Google APIs from the owner's Mac Mini. Selected content and instructions may be sent to the AI services configured in OpenClaw to fulfill requests. Outputs may be delivered through Discord and visible to authorized participants in the relevant conversation. These providers process that content under their respective policies and account settings. Google data is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally published on this website.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored locally using gog's configured credential storage, currently macOS Keychain. Conversation history, logs, reports, downloaded files, and generated documents may remain on the Mac Mini, in Discord, or in the connected Google account until the owner deletes them or applicable retention settings remove them. This integration has no separate public user database.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke access in Google Account connections, remove stored credentials with gog, and delete local files, conversation history, or generated Google files. Revoking authorization stops future authorized API access but does not automatically delete previously saved copies. For deletion requests or questions, contact tomraff.agent@gmail.com.

Limited use

The integration's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The integration does not use Google user data to develop or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

This information website

These static pages do not request Google authorization, collect Google account data, or run analytics scripts. They are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary request information under GitHub's privacy statement.
