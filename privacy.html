OpenClaw

Privacy policy

OpenClaw is a private assistant that connects to Google services with the account owner's authorization.

Information and use

The integration accesses authorized account information and content from Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, and Sheets to carry out requested tasks. Access is limited by the permissions granted during authorization.

Processing and storage

Information may be processed by configured AI and communication services when needed to fulfill requests. Credentials and task-related information may be retained in the integration's configured storage and connected services until removed by the owner or applicable retention settings.

Control

The account owner controls access and retention and can delete stored information or revoke Google access through Google Account settings. Revoking access does not automatically remove previously saved information.

Limited use

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising or training generalized AI models. Use and transfer of Google API data adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This static information website does not request account access or collect Google account content. Hosting providers may process standard web-request information.
