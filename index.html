PRIVATE PERSONAL INTEGRATION

OpenClaw personal assistant

This is the information page for a privately operated OpenClaw assistant running on its owner's Mac Mini. It is not a public service and is not the official website of the OpenClaw project.

Google account integration

The assistant uses the local gog command-line application to access Google services that its owner authorizes:

  • Gmail: find and read messages, create drafts, and send or organize email when requested.
  • Calendar: read schedules and create or update events.
  • Drive, Docs, and Sheets: locate, read, create, and edit files, documents, and spreadsheets.

Google access is granted through Google's authorization flow. The assistant does not ask for the owner's Google password. Access can be revoked at any time in Google Account settings.

Requests and selected Google content may be processed by the assistant's configured AI provider and may appear in its private Discord conversations. See the privacy policy for details.
